WHY I LIKE RUGBY

Rugby's the best sport in the world, It's not just a sport, it's a lifestyle.

  • The thrill of the tackle,
  • the rush of scoring a try,
  • the bond with your teammates,

It's unbeatable. I mean, who wouldn't love the physicality, strategy, and skill involved. From Springboks to local clubs, rugby's got a place for everyone. Whether you're a fly-half mastermind or a prop on the front row, it's a game that demands respect and rewards teamwork. Off the pitch, rugby's a community, mates, biltong, and epic stories. These are the reasons why I love and enjoy this sport so much
